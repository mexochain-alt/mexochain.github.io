FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MEXOTOKEN.

What is MEXOTOKEN?

MEXOTOKEN is a blockchain ecosystem built on The Open Network (TON). Its native digital asset, MEXO, is designed to support long-term ecosystem development, transparency and future Web3 applications.

Why was MEXO created?

MEXO was created to become the foundation of the MEXOTOKEN ecosystem, supporting future decentralized services while maintaining a transparent and sustainable economic model.

Which blockchain does MEXO use?

MEXO is deployed as a TON Jetton on The Open Network (TON), providing fast, secure and low-cost transactions.

Is the supply fixed?

Yes. The maximum supply is permanently limited to 11 Billion MEXO. No additional tokens can ever be created beyond the predefined supply.
